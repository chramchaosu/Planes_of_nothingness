A webcomic

How to read

Scroll shows the whole episode as one long strip. Just keep scrolling down.

Pages shows one page at a time. Swipe, or tap the right or left edge to turn the page.

Tap the middle of a picture to hide the bars and give it the whole screen. Tap again to bring them back. Wide pictures look biggest with the phone turned sideways.

Every episode opens on its first page. Episodes you've read to the end are marked as finished.
